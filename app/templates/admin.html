Account

Crossfact has one account: yours. Lost your authenticator, forgot the password, or locked out? From a machine with cluster access:

kubectl -n crossfact exec -it deploy/crossfact -- python -m app.admin reset-2fa

Also set-password, unlock, rename and status.
